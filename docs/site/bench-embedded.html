No server · one file in an app · 16 November 2026

One file in your app, against what apps embed today

Apps keep rows in SQLite, analytics in DuckDB, vectors in LanceDB or FAISS and search in Tantivy. ChloeDB does all of it in one file. Here ChloeDB 0.3.5 runs as a Python library against each, one at a time on one idle 8-vCPU node, all on 16 November.

First on hybrid and vector search, recommendations, list pages and reads beside a writer. Behind SQLite on single-row work and price updates, DuckDB on aggregates and the heavy join, and Tantivy on BM25 over the three smaller collections.

Search

Hybrid and vector search fastest on every collection

Hybrid search is 4.1 to 18× faster than the next system, with nDCG@10 within 0.021 of the best, and as fast held to one thread; under a filter it is 13 to 23× faster. Vectors are 7.8 to 11× faster than DuckDB's, the next fastest, at recall@10 0.996 to 1.000. BM25 is 1.6 and 1.7× faster than Tantivy on trec-covid and quora, and Tantivy 1.1 to 1.4× faster on the three smaller collections. Under each bar: nDCG@10, or recall@10 for vectors.

SQLite did not run on quora: three timed passes of its exact vector scans take about 9 hours. Its search rows are Debian's SQLite 3.40.1, its app and writes rows 3.53.1. Tantivy kept stopwords the others dropped; with the same stopwords its BM25 ties ChloeDB's on nDCG@10 (below).

The app

Pages and joins faster than SQLite; single rows slower

ChloeDB is 7.2 to 7.9× faster than SQLite at filtered, sorted pages, 3.7 to 4.1× at the heavy join, 5.0 to 6.1× at reads beside a writer, and 2.5× and 171× at the two aggregates. SQLite reads one row in 11 µs, against ChloeDB's 30 µs by key. DuckDB is fastest at aggregates and the heavy join. This node's disk is slow to sync, so every system that syncs commits single rows slowly here: SQLite at FULL 933 a second, against 5,093 on 2 November's node.

Writes and streams

Indexed writes, and reads beside a stream

With a text and a vector index, ChloeDB commits single rows 1.6× faster than SQLite at FULL (556 a second against 340), level with SQLite at NORMAL, and 2.7 to 6.5× faster than LanceDB and DuckDB. It takes 100-row batches fastest, LanceDB 10,000-row ones. SQLite at FULL updates prices 2.3× and vectors 2.5× faster. While 10,000 rows a second stream in, ChloeDB answers 5,758 reads a second, 8× DuckDB's and 14 to 16× SQLite's; SQLite keeps up with 7,087 to 7,327 of the 10,000 rows a second.

At 50,000 rows a second ChloeDB keeps up with 14,916 and answers 4,333 reads a second beside them; DuckDB keeps up with 44,533 and answers 314, and LanceDB with 49,869 and answers 2. LanceDB adds a fragment and a version with every write and nothing compacts during the run, so its updates take two thirds of a second each on average.

Recommendations

Similar items in under a millisecond, under filters

ChloeDB answers similar items in 0.92 ms, level with FAISS beside SQLite (0.99 ms), at the 99th percentile in 2.0 ms against 13.6, and 2.9× as many a second on four threads; LanceDB takes 21× longer and sqlite-vec's exact scan 615×. ChloeDB's home feed fuses vectors with a PageRank walk in one statement: 8.5 ms, 4.7× faster than LanceDB's with the walk in app code.

Against Tantivy

Tantivy as the app's store, and BM25 side by side

On a newer node (Xeon Gold 6548N) with ChloeDB of 3 November, not run again: ChloeDB commits single rows and updates 30× faster and reads rows faster; Tantivy aggregates, joins and bulk-loads faster. With the same stopwords their BM25 ties on nDCG@10.

Where ChloeDB is behind

Single rows, aggregates, price updates and a fast stream

  • Single rows. SQLite reads one by id in 11 µs against ChloeDB's 47 through SQL (30 by key), and joins a user's reviews in 28 µs against 654.
  • Single-row commits. 268 to 800 a second without indexes on this node, against SQLite's 933 to 1,340 at FULL. ChloeDB's log only grows, and on this disk a sync that grows a file takes twice one that overwrites; the 6 November build is as fast here.
  • Aggregates. DuckDB counts reviews per brand in 8.1 ms and per month in 11.7, against ChloeDB's 51.3 and 27.3 (SQLite 135 and 4,676). DuckDB's heavy join takes 302 ms, ChloeDB's 899.
  • Price updates. 528 a second without indexes against SQLite's 1,142 at FULL, and 552 with them against 1,286.
  • A fast stream. At 50,000 rows a second ChloeDB keeps up with 14,916, against DuckDB's 44,533 and the 6 November build's 45,713 on its node.
  • BM25 on small collections. Tantivy is 1.1 to 1.4× faster on scifact, nfcorpus and fiqa.
  • Against its 6 November build. Its searches take 1.35 to 2.3 times as long as that build's did on 6 November's node (vectors 2.9, at higher recall), more than the other systems slowed (1.16 to 1.42); the two builds have not searched side by side on one node yet.
