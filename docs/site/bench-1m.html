Cluster · 1M passages · DigitalOcean · 16 November 2026

Fourteen engines, 1M passages

ChloeDB 0.3.5 against thirteen engines, all in one run, with the same data, the same 14 kinds of query, three passes each and every engine's result cache off. Engines that shard had two 4-vCPU machines, the others one 8-vCPU machine, with the same CPU limit.

ChloeDB is first under all five ranking rules. It runs all 14 kinds and is in the top half on 42 of 45 tests; its lowest places are key lookups, 10th of 14 at the median and 11th at the 99th percentile. It is fastest outright on 14 tests: the three of SQL aggregates and of both hybrids, facets at the 99th percentile and at four clients, phrases and filtered vectors at the 99th percentile, and prefixes at four clients. Qdrant is faster on BM25, Meilisearch and Typesense on typos, Solr on prefixes at one client, and Redis on key lookups and vectors.

Data
1M MS MARCO v2.1 passages, with their Arctic Embed M v1.5 vectors (768 dimensions)
Machines
DigitalOcean sfo2, 16 November: two 4-vCPU nodes for each engine that shards, one 8-vCPU node for the others. Each lane's engines took turns on the same machines; Vespa ran in a lane of its own and Milvus alone on its own node
ChloeDB
0.3.5 (the tag's commit 1445c10, with the harness of #575): a shard and its coordinator run as one process per pod, and the coordinator answers its own shard in process
Budget
6.8 CPUs per engine. ChloeDB: 2 pods of 3.4 CPUs, each a shard and a coordinator in one process, like Elasticsearch's data nodes
Queries
300 dev queries per kind at 1, 4 and 16 clients; the median of three passes
Quality
Recall@10 against an exact search of the same vectors
Noise
ChloeDB ran first and again last on the same machines, hours apart: the second round was 2.3% slower (the median over 42 timings, up to 9.9%)
Cost
$28.67 at list price, with the 10M head-to-head

Recall against speed

With a filter, 2.6 to 6.2 times Qdrant's queries a second

The rankings use 64 candidates (ef) for every engine, where ChloeDB's vector recall is 0.809 and Qdrant's 0.909. Both ran again at 64, 128, 256 and 512 candidates. Without a filter Qdrant answers a single query sooner at its first setting (1.90 ms at recall 0.896, against ChloeDB's 2.34 at 0.875); above that ChloeDB is ahead: 2.66 ms and 1,511 queries a second at 0.931 against Qdrant's 2.83 ms and 1,068 at 0.944, and 3.33 ms and 1,055 at 0.957 against 4.62 ms and 622 at 0.965. Qdrant goes on to 0.984 at 512 candidates, where ChloeDB stops at 0.957. With a filter ChloeDB serves 2.6 to 6.2 times as many queries a second at about equal recall and is faster at one client at every setting. Elasticsearch, OpenSearch, Solr and Vespa ran the same sweep: at 512 candidates they reach 0.900, 0.766, 0.939 and 0.944 (ef.md).

ChloeDB on two m-4vcpu-32gb nodes (two pods of 3.4 CPUs, Xeon Platinum 8168 and 8358), Qdrant on one m-8vcpu-64gb node (8168), 16 November; medians of three passes.

Writes and streams

Searchable in 7 ms, and a stream written once

ChloeDB and Vespa make a write searchable when it returns (7.4 and 10.9 ms); Elasticsearch, OpenSearch and Solr refresh every second. At 16 clients ChloeDB's single inserts are a little faster than Elasticsearch's and a little slower than OpenSearch's and Solr's, and its updates are 2.4 times as fast as any other's, because it sets one column in place. After a consumer crash, ChloeDB resumes exactly, since offsets commit with the rows; the others write some events twice. None lost any.

Load and size

Shown, not ranked

ChloeDB's shards were built offline from local files, the others over HTTP, so load times do not compare.

Where ChloeDB is behind

Three kinds where it is clearly slower

  • Typo search. 16.5 ms, down from 120 in the first run (#242, #266) but up from 11.9 with 7 November's image; Meilisearch takes 6.9, Typesense 7.2 and Elasticsearch 14.6.
  • Key lookups. 1.03 ms through a coordinator, 10th of 14; Redis takes 0.23 and Postgres 0.36. Half the keys are the coordinator's own shard's and skip the network (#299), but the median key is one of the other half.
  • BM25 × recency. 3.71 ms, 4th; Qdrant takes 2.21. Plain BM25 is 2nd at 2.03 ms, behind Qdrant's 1.89.
Since the first run

SQL aggregates 24 times faster, typos seven times, BM25 2.1 times the queries at 16 clients

ChloeDB's medians in the first 1M run (3 November) and on 0.3.5. The coordinator caches each shard version's term statistics (#245), so a BM25 query takes one round to the shards instead of two; a misspelt word is no longer expanded against the whole dictionary (#242, #266); aggregates read their groups from the columns (#252, #264); and later BM25 segments start at the scores the earlier ones found (#258). The machines changed too: Elasticsearch's BM25 median went from to ms, OpenSearch's from to . One-client latency also pays the network hop to a coordinator in a shard's pod (#287), which the first run's coordinator, on the client's machine, did not.
